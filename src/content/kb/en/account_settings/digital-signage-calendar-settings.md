This setting allows you to upload a logo for the Door Label app. The logo will be displayed on the door label screens, helping you maintain consistent branding across your meeting room or room signage displays.

When uploading a logo, make sure it follows the recommended size limits to ensure it displays correctly on the screens.

**Logo requirements:**

-   Maximum width: 600px
    
-   Maximum height: 300px
    

Once uploaded, the logo will automatically be used by the Door Label application on supported screens.
