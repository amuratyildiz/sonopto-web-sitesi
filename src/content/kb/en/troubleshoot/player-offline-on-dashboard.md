## Player Connected to Internet but Showing Offline on Dashboard

If your player has internet access but appears **Offline** in the dashboard, check the following:

1.  Make sure the device **date and time** are correct.
2.  Make sure the device **timezone** is correct.
3.  Some devices get stuck on timezone sync. Switch to a **nearby timezone** and test again.
4.  Connect the player to a **mobile hotspot** to quickly test network restrictions.
5.  If it comes online on hotspot, your regular internet is likely blocking the digital signage traffic.

Correct date/time and unrestricted network access should bring the player online in the dashboard.
