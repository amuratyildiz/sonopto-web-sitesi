-   Open an existing playlist or create a new one
-   From the layout preview, click on one of the layers to start adding content
-   Each layer has its own media apps depending on its size (width and height)
-   If the layer is empty, the Apps dialog will open

<img src="/kb/playlists/digital-signage-apps.webp" alt="Digital Signage Apps" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

-   Use the “View Toggle Button” in the top-right corner to change the view layout of the apps and switch between tabs.

<img src="/kb/playlists/digital-signage-apps-view.webp" alt="Digital Signage Apps View" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

-   Click on an app to add it to the layer playlist

_Note: To find out how each app works go to [](/en/support/apps/)Apps Section_

### Search Apps

-   Use the **Search Apps** bar to filter the apps
-   Click on the app in the search result drop down
-   Start configuring your app

### Layer’s Transition

<img src="/kb/playlists/layer_transition.webp" alt="Layer Transition" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

-   To change the transition type, choose one from the drop down.
-   Layer transition will override playlist transition  
    _Check your screen for preview, this is similar to a slideshow’s transition from a media to another._

### Add New

<img src="/kb/playlists/add_new.webp" alt="Add New" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

-   To add more content to the layer, click on the **Add New** button. Choose an app from the list.
-   Below the Add New button are listed all the media items added to this layer
-   Each media is represented by a preview, a name and the duration of playback
-   **Order**: Drag and drop an item from the list to change its position in the layer playlist. This is the order of playback on the screen
-   In the middle of the screen, you can preview the media item selected from the list
-   **Edit**: Click on Edit on the top left corner of the media preview to manage media playback options. You can also hover the media item in the list and click on Edit.
-   **Delete**: click on the delete icon on the top right of the preview to remove this media from the layer
