A Channel is a collection of playlists that can be used to create your own content channel by reusing playlists.

### Step 1: Create a Channel

<img src="/kb/playlists/channel1.webp" alt="Channels" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

-   Go to the Playlists page
-   Click on **Channels** Tab
-   Click on **Add Channel**

<img src="/kb/playlists/add-channel.webp" alt="Add Channel" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

-   In the “Add Channel” box, click on “Name” field and write the name of the channel you want to create.
-   Choose the Screen Orientation from the drop down list.
-   Select a “Team” you want to restrict the access to.  
    (Teams must be created before adding a Channel. To add Teams and give access to channels, check [Teams](/en/support/user-management/teams/) in **User Management** )
-   Click on “ADD”.
-   Once you click ADD, another screen will open to assign playlists to the channel you created.  
    ([Playlists](/en/support/playlists/) must be created before adding Channels)

<img src="/kb/playlists/add-playlists.webp" alt="Add Playlists" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

-   Available Playlists will be shown on the right side of the screen.  
    (If you choose Horizontal as a channel screen orientation, only horizontal playlists will appear. If you choose Vertical as a channel screen orientation, only vertical playlists will appear).
-   Click on the **+** sign to add playlists to the channel.
-   The assigned playlists will appear on the left side of the screen.

<img src="/kb/playlists/add-playlists2.webp" alt="Add Playlists" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

-   To delete an assigned playlist, Click on “delete” next to the playlist.
-   In the Assigned Playlists List, you can change the Playlist position by Drag and Drop. Just click-hold the playlist and move it upwards or downwards to the desired place.
-   Once you finish assigning playlists to the channel, click on **Close**.
-   To edit Playlists in the Channel, click on the “PLAYLISTS” column next to the channels name.

<img src="/kb/playlists/add-playlists3.webp" alt="Add Playlists" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

### Step 2: Add a Channel to a Screen

-   Go to the “SCREENS” page.
-   Click on Media Source(Source Type).

<img src="/kb/playlists/add-screen1.webp" alt="Add Channel to Screen" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

-   “Choose Source” screen will open.

<img src="/kb/playlists/add-screen2.webp" alt="Add Channel to Screen" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

-   Click on “CHANGE SOURCE TYPE”.
-   From the “Choose Source Type” screen, select “**Channels**”.

<img src="/kb/playlists/add-screen3.webp" alt="Add Channel to Screen" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

-   Choose the Channel Name you want to display on the screen.

<img src="/kb/playlists/add-screen4.webp" alt="Add Channel to Screen" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

-   Click on **Publish**.
-   Your Channel will download and play on your screen.
