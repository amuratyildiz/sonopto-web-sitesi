Auto start is the ability of the software to automatically start playing the content as soon as the device is powered on or restarted. It helps businesses keep their digital signage displays up and running without requiring manual intervention.

-   Go to screen settings

<img src="/destek/installation/open-settings.webp" alt="Android TV open settings" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

-   Go to Apps Or Google Play Store

<img src="/destek/installation/auto-start-go-to-apps.webp" alt="Go to apps settings" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

-   Go to “**Special app access**”

<img src="/destek/installation/go-to-special-permission.webp" alt="Android TV soecial permission" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

-   Go to “**Display over other apps**”

<img src="/destek/installation/go-to-display-over-other-apps.webp" alt="Android TV display over other apps" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

-   Enable “**Allow display over other apps**” for the digital signage app.

<img src="/destek/installation/go-to-enable-over-other-apps-no-branding.webp" alt="Android TV enable display over other apps" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

-   All done. Try restarting your screen. The digital signage app should auto-start after a few seconds from the device boot.
