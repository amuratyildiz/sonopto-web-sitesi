_This Guide is only intended for Android-based Vestel digital signage screens._

### Step 1: Get the APK file

-   Download the latest APK from [https://download.cloud-digitalsignage.com/signage-latest.apk](/iletisim/)
    
-   Copy it to a USB disk.
    
-   Plug the USB disk into the TV, locate the USB port on your Vestel TV depending on your model.
    
-   Press the power button to turn on the TV.
    

### Step 2: Vestel TV Setup Wizard

-   When the TV boots for the first time, using the remote control or using a keyboard, choose your preferred language.

<img src="/kb/installation/1-vestel-language.webp" alt="Language" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

-   Choose your Country, or Other.

<img src="/kb/installation/2-vestel-country.webp" alt="Country" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

-   Choose Skip Pin.

<img src="/kb/installation/3-vestel-skip-pin.webp" alt="Pin" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

-   Choose your preferred **Orientation**, then Next.

<img src="/kb/installation/4-vestel-signage-settings.webp" alt="Signage Settings" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

-   Keep Auto Launch _Disabled_ for now.

<img src="/kb/installation/5-vestel-auto-launch.webp" alt="Auto Launch" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

-   Set **Power Mode** to **Reactive**, this is an important step if Power Management is needed.

<img src="/kb/installation/6-vestel-operation-mode.webp" alt="Operation Mode" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

-   Choose **Network Type** (Wired or Wireless).

<img src="/kb/installation/7-vestel-network.webp" alt="Network" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

-   If You choose wireless, setup your wifi settings,
-   Set **Networked Standby Mode** to **On**, then Next.
-   For Manufacturer Usage and Diagnostics, Click Accept or Skip.

<img src="/kb/installation/8-vestel-manufacturer.webp" alt="Manufacturer" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

### Step 3: Install from USB

-   After the setup wizard, in the menu, navigate to and press on **Applications**.

<img src="/kb/installation/9-vestel-menu.webp" alt="Menu" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

-   Choose **AppInstaller**

<img src="/kb/installation/10-vestel-app-installer.webp" alt="App Installer" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

-   Choose your USB device.

<img src="/kb/installation/11-vestel-usb.webp" alt="USB" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

-   Choose **Digital Signage** file

<img src="/kb/installation/15-vestel-ds.webp" alt="sonopto.signage" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

-   Press on Install

<img src="/kb/installation/16-vestel-install-ds.webp" alt="Install" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

-   Press on **Open**

### Step 4: Power Up Settings

-   Press on Menu from the remote control

<img src="/kb/installation/17-vestel-power-up.webp" alt="Power Up Menu" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

-   Choose Power Up Settings
    
-   Make sure Power Mode is set to **Reactive**
    
-   Choose **Auto Launch**
    

<img src="/kb/installation/18-vestel-autolaunch.webp" alt="Auto Launch" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

-   Choose **Select Application**, then **Digital Signage**

<img src="/kb/installation/19-vestel-select-app.webp" alt="Power up sonopto.signage" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

### Step 5: Display Hash

-   Open the digital signage application
    
-   Wait for a few seconds until the screen ID shows as below
    
-   Use the displayed hash (screen ID) to register the display on your CMS web portal.
