This guide offers clear instructions for installing Digital Signage on TCL commercial Android displays, configuring settings, and setting the digital signage app as the default playback application.

## Supported TCL Digital Signage Models

-   TMN series (Android 13)
-   TBN series (Android 13)
-   TEN series (Android 9)

## How to Install Digital Signage on TCL Commercial Displays

### Step 1: Get the APK file

-   Insert a USB drive into a computer
    
-   Download the latest APK from [**Download APK**](/iletisim/)
    
-   Copy it to the main directory of your USB drive
    
-   Insert the USB drive into an available USB port on your TCL digital signage display
    

### Step 2: Install the APK file

-   On your remote control, press the **Apps** button

<img src="/kb/installation/tcl-remotecontrol.webp" alt="TCL Remote Control" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

-   Go to **File Browser**

<img src="/kb/installation/tcl-install-apk.webp" alt="TCL Apps" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

-   Click on the **APK** file
    
-   Choose **Install**
    
-   Wait for the installation process to complete, then click **Done**
    

### Step 3: Set Digital Signage as the Home App (Primary CMS)

-   Press the **Settings** button on your remote control
    
-   Go to **system** –> **customisation** –> **primary cms**
    
-   Select the digital signage app to set it as the primary CMS for your TV
    

### Step 4: Set the Timezone

-   Go to **date and time**
    
-   Make sure the date and time are correct.
    

### Step 5: Configure Auto-Start

Auto start allows the digital signage software to launch automatically when the device powers on or restarts.

-   **Step 1**:

(This step depends on your TV specs)

Go to Settings > Device Preferences > Quick Start > Toggle the Quick Start switch to the On position.

or:

Go to Settings > Apps > Special app access > Display over other apps > Ensure that the “the sonopto.signage app” is ON.

-   **Step 2**:
    
    -   Go to the “Safety Guard” app on your TV.
    -   Select “Permission shield”.
    -   Select “Auto launch permission”.
    -   First, toggle “Auto manager” to Closed.
    -   then, toggle “the sonopto.signage app” to Opened.
-   **Step 3**:
    
    -   Go to Settings > Device Preferences > Restart.
    -   After the TV powers back, the digital signage app should start automatically after a few seconds.

### Step 6: Configure Power Management

To enable the power management feature:

-   With the digital signage app running, connect a keyboard to the display
    
-   Press **ALT + M** to open the hidden menu
    
-   Enter password: **1122**
    
-   Press **Open TV Settings**
    
-   Navigate to **Power Key Definition** and set it to **Sleep Now**
    

**Note**: The power management feature is only available for Android rooted devices.

### Step 7: Register Your Screen

-   Restart the display
    
-   The digital signage app will launch automatically and show a **Screen ID**
    
-   Go to the CMS, navigate to **Screens**, and click **Add Screen**
    
-   Enter the **Screen ID** to link the display to your account
