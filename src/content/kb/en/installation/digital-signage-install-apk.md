## How to Install Android APK on Your Digital Signage Device

Follow these steps to install the APK on your Android digital signage device:

### Step 1: Download the APK

1.  **Sign in** to your digital signage portal.
2.  Navigate to the **Screens** section.
3.  Click on **Add Screen**.
4.  Download the Android APK from the **left banner** as shown in the image below:

<img src="/kb/installation/apk-download.webp" alt="Copy google sheet url and add it to digital signage" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

* * *

### Step 2: Transfer the APK to a USB Flash Drive

1.  Copy the downloaded APK file to a **USB flash drive**.
2.  Ensure the USB flash drive is formatted correctly (e.g., FAT32 or NTFS) and the APK file is successfully transferred.

* * *

### Step 3: Connect the USB Flash Drive to Your Android Device

1.  Insert the USB flash drive into the **USB port** of your Android digital signage device.

* * *

### Step 4: Enable Installation from Unknown Sources

1.  On your Android device, navigate to **Settings**.
2.  Go to **Security** (or **Privacy**, depending on the device).
3.  Enable the option **Install from Unknown Sources** or **Allow Unknown Apps**.
    -   If prompted, confirm the security warning.

* * *

### Step 5: Install the APK

1.  Open the **File Manager** on your Android device.
2.  Locate and select the APK file on the USB flash drive.
3.  Tap **Install**.
4.  Wait for the installation to complete.

* * *

### Step 6: Launch the Application

1.  Once installed, open the app directly or locate it in your app list.
2.  Follow any on-screen instructions for initial setup.

* * *

### Step 7: Assign the Device to Your Account

1.  After launching the application, a **hash code** will appear on the screen.
2.  Copy this hash code.
3.  Go back to your digital signage portal.
4.  Use the hash code to add the device to your account and complete the setup.
