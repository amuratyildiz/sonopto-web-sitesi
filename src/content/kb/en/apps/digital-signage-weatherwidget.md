The WeatherWidget app is a customizable tool designed to display real-time weather information directly on your digital signage.

<video width="90%" src="/destek/apps/weather-widget-app.mp4" controls="" preload="metadata" playsinline=""></video>

-   Go to the [](https://weatherwidget.io)WeatherWidget app.
-   Select the location to view the weather.
-   Customise the weather widget to match your brand. Choose your themes and colours.
-   Click on the “GET CODE” button.
-   Click “COPY TO CLIPBOARD” to copy the code.
-   Now, go to the management console.
-   Click on the playlist, then the layer where you want to add the Weatherwidget app.
-   If the layer has no media, the apps dialogue will open. Otherwise, click on Add New.
-   Go to “General Apps”.
-   Choose “WeatherWidget”.
-   Paste the copied HTML Embed Code in the designated field.
-   Click “Save” and then “Publish”.

**Note: When adjusting the width of the widget, ensure that the screen layer is also set to the same width. For example, if the widget width is 800px, make sure to embed it in a layer that is also 800px wide.**
