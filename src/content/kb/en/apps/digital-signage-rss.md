-   Go to the RSS web page and copy the RSS feed URL (_e.g. [https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/feeds/news/](https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/feeds/news/)_).
-   Go to the Management Console.
-   Click on the playlist then the layer where you will add RSS.
-   If the layer has no media, the apps dialog will open. Otherwise, click on **Add New**.
-   Click on **News Apps**.
-   Click on **RSS**.
-   Paste the URL in the RSS URL box.
-   Select a theme: Theme 1, Theme 2, or Images Only.  
    _Images Only will only get images without displaying the news headline and description_
-   Select Headline color, Description Color, and Background Color.
-   Choose the stories count (how many stories to get).
-   Wait for the stories to be processed.
-   Check the stories and use the arrows to navigate left and right through them.
