The Door Label app displays the room’s status and upcoming meetings and events from your Microsoft and Google Calendar.

-   Go to the **Playlists** page.
-   Select Horizontal orientation tab for your playlist.
-   Click on **ADD PLAYLIST**.

<img src="/kb/apps/add-playlist.webp" alt="Add Playlist" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

-   **Add Playlist** and fill up the name of the playlist.
-   Select a **Team** from the drop-down list. (Teams must be created before creating playlists. Check [Teams](/en/support/user-management/teams/) in **User Management**)
-   Click **Save**.

<img src="/kb/apps/add-playlist1.webp" alt="Add Playlist" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

-   Choose Full Layout 1 for your Door Label (1920x1080 px).
-   Click on the layer to add media.

<img src="/kb/apps/add-playlist.2.webp" alt="Add Playlist" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

-   From the Apps Page, select **“Calendars”**

<img src="/kb/apps/select-door-label.webp" alt="Select Door Label" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

-   Choose one of the calendars: **Microsoft Door Label** or **Google Door Label**.

<img src="/kb/apps/select-calendar.webp" alt="Select Calendar" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

### If you choose Microsoft Door Label:

-   Fill in the details for the account you want to connect.

<img src="/kb/apps/add-account.webp" alt="Add Account" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

-   Click on ADD/REFRESH ACCOUNT.
-   A login page will open to sign into your Microsoft Account.

<img src="/kb/apps/sign-in.webp" alt="Sign in" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

-   Enter your email address.
-   Click Next.
-   Enter your email password.
-   click sign in.

<img src="/kb/apps/account-login.webp" alt="Account Login" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

-   Your Microsoft Account is now connected.
-   From the drop-down list, select a calendar.
-   Select a language for your Door Label.
-   Click Save.

<img src="/kb/apps/door-label.webp" alt="Door Label" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

-   Click on **“Show Upcoming Events”** to see what’s next on your calendar.

<img src="/kb/apps/door-label2.webp" alt="Door Label" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

-   When it is time for the event on your Calendar, your door label will change from vacant to occupied.

<img src="/kb/apps/occupied.webp" alt="Door Label Occupied" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

-   To update your door label for any changes in your calendar, you need to refresh your account.
-   Go to the door label layer.
-   Click on **EDIT**
-   Click **ADD/REFRESH ACCOUNT** in the media settings.
-   Click Save.

<img src="/kb/apps/update-account.webp" alt="Update Account" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

-   To delete an account, click on **EDIT**.
-   Click on **REMOVE** in media settings.

**To add another Microsoft account to your Door Label:**

-   Sign out from your Microsoft account.
-   Click on Edit in the Door Label Layer.
-   Click again on ADD/REFRESH ACCOUNT.
-   Log in Page will open.
-   Sign in to another Microsoft Account.
-   Enter the email Address and password and then click Sign in.
-   The 2nd Microsoft Account is now connected to your Door Label.
-   Select a Calendar for the new Microsoft Account added.
-   From the **Account** drop-down list in the media settings, you can now switch between accounts.

<img src="/kb/apps/2nd-account.webp" alt="Microsoft Account" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

### If you choose Google Door Label:

-   Click on “ADD/REFRESH ACCOUNT”.

<img src="/kb/apps/add-google-account.webp" alt="Add Google Account" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

-   Sign in with your Google Account.
-   Click on “Continue” to allow digital signage to access your Google Account.
-   Click on “Allow” to allow digital signage to see and download your Google Calendar.
-   Your Google Account is now connected to the door label app.

<img src="/kb/apps/google-account-login.webp" alt="Google Account Login" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

-   Choose a calendar from the drop down list, then click on SAVE.

<img src="/kb/apps/google-account-vacant.webp" alt="Google Account Vacant" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

-   You can click on “Show Upcoming Events” to see what’s next on you calendar.
-   When it’s time for the event on your calendar, your door label status will change from vacant to occupied.
-   To update your door label for any changes in the calendar, you need to refresh your account.
-   To delete an account, click on Edit, and then click **Remove** in media settings.

**To add another Google Account to your Door Label:**

-   Click on Edit in the Door Label Layer.
-   Click on ADD/REFRESH ACCOUNT.
-   “Sign in with google” Page will open.
-   Enter the email address and password and then click Continue.
-   Click on “Allow” to allow digital signage to see and download the Google Calendar.
-   Select a Calendar for the new Google Account added.
-   From the **Account** drop-down list in the media settings, you can now switch between accounts.

### To add a logo to your Door Label:

-   Click on the profile icon on the top right corner of your page.
-   Go to **Settings**.
-   Go to **Calendars** tab.
-   Click on **UPLOAD LOGO** to upload a logo to be used by the door label app. (Door Label logo must be Max width: 600px - Max height 300 px)

<img src="/kb/apps/logo.webp" alt="Door Label Logo" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

-   Browse your files and choose the logo you want to upload.
-   Once uploading is complete, click on **Done**.
-   To Edit your logo, click on Edit -> Click on Replace Logo -> Choose the logo you want to upload -> Done.
-   Go to the Door Label playlist and check your logo.

<img src="/kb/apps/logo2.webp" alt="Door Label Logo" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

### To display your Door Label on your Screen:

-   Go to **SCREENS** page.
-   Click on the Media Source(Source Type) on your Screen.
-   Choose “Playlists” from the Source Type page.
-   Click on Door Label Playlist.
-   Click **Publish**.
-   Now, your events will display on your door label screen.
