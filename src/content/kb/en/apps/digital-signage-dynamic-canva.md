-   Go to your [](https://www.canva.com)Canva design.
-   Click on the “Share” button in the top right corner.
-   Choose “Embed” from the list.
-   Click on the “Embed” button.
-   Click “Copy” next to the “Smart embed link”.
-   Now, go to the management console.
-   Click on the playlist, then the layer where you want to add the Dynamic Canva app.
-   If the layer has no media, the apps dialogue will open. Otherwise, click on Add New.
-   Go to “General Apps”.
-   Choose “**Dynamic Canva**”.
-   Paste the copied link.
-   Check the “Enable” checkbox to allow the web app to auto-reload every X minutes. Specify the desired refresh interval in minutes.
-   Click “Save” and then “Publish”.

Now, any updates to your Canva design will automatically reflect on your digital signage screen within the refresh rate minutes.

_**Please note: The Dynamic Canva app requires a stable internet connection to sync updates—offline use is not supported.**_

**Note:** The key difference between the Canva App and the Dynamic Canva App lies in how updates are managed. With the Canva App, users design their content within the digital signage dashboard and then manually save and publish it to the screens. Any future changes to the design require re-saving and re-publishing to update the screens. In contrast, the Dynamic Canva App allows users to embed a live Canva link directly into the digital signage app. This means any edits made in Canva are automatically reflected on the screens—no need to manually update or publish the content again.
