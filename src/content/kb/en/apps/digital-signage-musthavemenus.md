MustHaveMenus is an all-in-one menu design and marketing platform that enables users to create, manage, and distribute menus and promotional materials across digital signage channels.

<video width="90%" src="/destek/apps/musthavemenus-app.mp4" controls="" preload="metadata" playsinline=""></video>

-   Go to the [](https://www.musthavemenus.com)MusthaveMenus app.
-   Choose your menu board design.
-   Click on the “Share” button in the top right corner.
-   Choose “View Only Link” from the options available.
-   Click on the “Copy” button to copy the link.
-   Now, go to the management console.
-   Click on the playlist, then the layer where you want to add the MusthaveMenus app.
-   If the layer has no media, the apps dialogue will open. Otherwise, click on Add New.
-   Go to “General Apps”.
-   Choose “MusthaveMenus”.
-   Paste the copied URL link in the designated field.
-   Specify the desired refresh interval in minutes to allow the web app to auto-reload every X minutes.
-   Click “Save” and then “Publish”.

Now, any updates to your Musthavemenus design will automatically reflect on your digital signage screen within the refresh rate minutes.

_**Note: After making changes or updates to your design, click the publish button in the musthavemenus app in the top right corner. This ensures the refresh rate works and updates are reflected on your screen.**_
