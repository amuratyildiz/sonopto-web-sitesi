The Outlook Calendar app seamlessly syncs with your calendar to display upcoming schedules and events.

-   Open the management console.
-   Select the playlist and navigate to the layer where you want to add Outlook Calendar.
-   If the layer has no media, the apps dialogue will open. Otherwise, click on **Add New**.
-   From the Apps page, select **Calendars**.

<img src="/destek/apps/digital-signage-calendar.webp" alt="Digital Signage Calendar" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

-   Click on **Outlook Calendar**.

<img src="/destek/apps/outlook-calendar.webp" alt="Outlook Calendar" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

-   Click on **ADD/REFRESH ACCOUNT**.
-   Sign in to your Microsoft Account to grant access to the app.
-   Enter your email address and password, then click **Sign in**.
-   Your Microsoft Account is now connected.

<img src="/destek/apps/sign-in-outlook-calendar.webp" alt="Sign in Microsoft Account" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

-   From the drop-down list, choose a calendar you wish to display.
-   In the Type field, choose how you want to view your calendar: by day, week, or month.

<img src="/destek/apps/calendar-view.webp" alt="Microsoft Calendar View" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

_**Weekly View:**_

<img src="/destek/apps/outlook-calendar-app.webp" alt="Microsoft Calendar the sonopto.signage app" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

-   You can also select the “List View” to display tasks in a list format. Enable “Auto-Scroll Interval” and set the duration in seconds for automatic page scrolling.
-   Click **Save**.

_**List View:**_

<img src="/destek/apps/outlook-calendar-list-view.webp" alt="Microsoft Calendar List View" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

-   When you finish editing, click on **Publish** to display the Outlook Calendar view on your screen.
