-   Copy your IPTV stream URL
-   Go to the Management Console
-   Click on the playlist then the layer where you will add IPTV
-   If the layer has no media, the apps dialog will open. Otherwise, click on **Add New**
-   Click on **Video Apps**
-   Click on **IPTV Stream**
-   Paste the copied URL in the box
-   Click on **Save**
