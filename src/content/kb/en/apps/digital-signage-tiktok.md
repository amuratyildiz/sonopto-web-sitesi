-   Go to TikTok and choose the video you want to show.
-   Click on **Share**.
-   Click on **Copy** to copy the link.
-   Go to the Management Console.
-   Click on the playlist then the layer where you will add TikTok.
-   If the layer has no media, the apps dialog will open. Otherwise, click on **Add New**
-   Click on **Social Media**.
-   Click on **TikTok**.
-   Paste the link you just copied.
-   Click on Save.
