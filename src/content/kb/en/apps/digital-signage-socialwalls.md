The Social Walls app is a platform that enables users to aggregate and display real-time social media content from various platforms such as Facebook, Twitter, Instagram, YouTube, and Pinterest.

<video width="90%" src="/kb/apps/social-walls-app.mp4" controls="" preload="metadata" playsinline=""></video>

-   Go to the [](https://socialwalls.com)Social walls app.
-   Choose the wall you want to share.
-   Click on the “Display” button at the bottom left corner.
-   Select platform “Digital Display”.
-   Then choose “Digital Signage”.
-   Go to “Others”.
-   Click on the generated link to copy it.
-   Now, go to the management console.
-   Click on the playlist, then the layer where you want to add the Social Walls app.
-   If the layer has no media, the apps dialogue will open. Otherwise, click on Add New.
-   Go to “General Apps”.
-   Choose “SocialWalls”.
-   Paste the copied URL link in the designated field.
-   Check the “Enable” checkbox to allow the app to auto-reload every X minutes. Specify the desired refresh interval in minutes.
-   Check the “Enable” checkbox to allow zooming in the app. Use the scroll feature right and left to zoom in and out (%).
-   Click “Save” and then “Publish”.
