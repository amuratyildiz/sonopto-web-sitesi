-   Go to the Management Console.
-   Click on the playlist then the layer where you will add Pexels.
-   If the layer has no media, the apps dialog will open. Otherwise, click on **Add New**
-   Click on **Image Libraries**.
-   Click on **Pexels**
-   Type your search text in the search box and hit **Enter**
-   Choose an image from the results and click on it to add to your playlist
