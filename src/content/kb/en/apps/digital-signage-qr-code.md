-   Go to the Management Console.
-   Click on the playlist then the layer where you want to add a QR Code
-   If the layer has no media, the apps dialog will open. Otherwise, click on **Add New**
-   Click on **Add from Templates**
-   Choose a Template category
-   Click on a template to add it
-   Wait for the template to be processed
-   Click on **Template Editor**
-   Click on **Objects**
-   Click on the qr code image
-   Enter the QR Code Link
-   Choose a foreground and a background for the generated QR Code
-   Click on **Done**
-   Adjust your QR Code position and size
-   Click on **Save**
