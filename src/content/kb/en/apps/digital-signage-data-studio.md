### Step 1: Make Report Visible and Share it

-   Go to Google Looker Studio Report.
-   At the top right, click on **Share**.
-   For Link settings, click on the drop down and choose **Public**.
-   Click on **Save** then **Close**.

### Step 2: Enable Embeding in Your Report

-   Click on **Edit** Report.
-   Click on **File** on the top left corner of your report.
-   Click on **Embed Report**.
-   Check **Enable embedding**.
-   Click on **Embed URL**
-   Click on **Copy to Clipboard**

If Embed Report is already enabled in your report:

-   Go to the top right corner of your report.
-   Click on the **drop down list** next to the Share button.
-   Choose **Embed report**.
-   Click on **Embed URL**.
-   Click on **COPY TO CLIPBOARD**.

### Step 3: Add Google Looker Studio to Digital Signage

-   Go to the Management Console.
-   Click on the playlist then the layer where you will add the Looker Studio Report.
-   If the layer has no media, the apps dialog will open. Otherwise, click on **Add New**.
-   Click on **Business Intelligence**.
-   Click on **Google Looker Studio**.
-   Paste the copied URL.
-   Click **Save** and preview the report.
