## 1\. Weather

<video width="90%" src="/destek/apps/digital-signage-weather.mp4" controls="" preload="metadata" playsinline=""></video>

-   Go to the Management Console.
-   Click on the playlist then the layer where you want to add a Weather
-   If the layer has no media, the apps dialog will open. Otherwise, click on **Add New**
-   Click on **Weather Apps**
-   Click on **Weather**
-   Type in the location
-   Choose the unit: Celcius or Fahrenheit
-   Click on **Save**
