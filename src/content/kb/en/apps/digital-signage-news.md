-   Go to the Management Console.
-   Click on the playlist then the layer where you will add News App.
-   If the layer has no media, the apps dialog will open. Otherwise, click on **Add New**.
-   Click on **News Apps**.
-   Click on one of the preset news apps.
-   Choose one of the topics from the drop down(news, sports, entertainment, countries…), it depends on each RSS provider.
-   Select a theme.
-   Select Headline color, Description Color, and Background Color.
-   Choose Stories count (how many stories to get).
-   Click on **Save**.
-   Wait for the stories to be processed.
-   Check the stories and use the arrows on the sides to navigate left and right through them.
