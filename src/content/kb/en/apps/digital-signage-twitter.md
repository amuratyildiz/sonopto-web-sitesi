-   Go to the management console
-   Click on the playlist then the layer where you will add twitter.
-   If the layer has no media, the apps dialog will open. Otherwise, click on **Add New**
-   Click on **Social Media**
-   Click on **Twitter**
-   Type in the twitter account (without @)
-   Choose the number of tweets
-   Click on **Save**
-   When the tweets are ready, you can view them
