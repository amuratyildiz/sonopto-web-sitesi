The Google Calendar app seamlessly syncs with your calendar to display upcoming schedules and events.

-   Open the management console.
-   Select the playlist and navigate to the layer where you want to add Google Calendar.
-   If the layer has no media, the apps dialogue will open. Otherwise, click on **Add New**.
-   From the Apps page, select **Calendars**.

<img src="/kb/apps/digital-signage-calendar.webp" alt="Digital Signage Calendar" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

-   Click on **Google Calendar**.

<img src="/kb/apps/google-calendar.webp" alt="Google Calendar" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

-   Click on **ADD/REFRESH ACCOUNT**.
-   Sign in to your Google Account to grant access to the app, then click “Continue”.

<img src="/kb/apps/signin-google-account.webp" alt="Sign in Google Calendar" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

-   Digital Signage wants to access your account > Click “Allow” to give access to the app to see your Google Calendar.

<img src="/kb/apps/give-access-google-account.webp" alt="Access to Google Calendar" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

-   Your Google Account is now connected.
-   From the drop-down list, choose a calendar you wish to display.

<img src="/kb/apps/google-account-connected.webp" alt="Google Calendar connected" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

-   In the Type field, choose how you want to view your calendar: by day, week, or month.
    
-   You can also select the “List View” to display tasks in a list format. Enable “Auto-Scroll Interval” and set the duration in seconds for automatic page scrolling.
    
-   Click **Save**.
