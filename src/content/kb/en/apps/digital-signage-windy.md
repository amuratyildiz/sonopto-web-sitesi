Windy.com is a comprehensive weather forecasting platform offering interactive maps and real-time data on wind, temperature, precipitation, and more.

<video width="90%" src="/kb/apps/windy.com-app.mp4" controls="" preload="metadata" playsinline=""></video>

-   Go to [](https://www.windy.com)windy.com app.
-   Choose the location to view the weather.
-   Navigate to the Menu bar.
-   Scroll down and click on “Embed Widget”.
-   Configure your map. Enter the width and height. For example, if you want to display the app on a full layer, you must enter width=1920 and height=1080. The size of the app depends on the size of the layer on the screen.
-   Click on the “Copy to clipboard” button.
-   Now, go to the management console.
-   Click on the playlist, then the layer where you want to add the Windy.com app.
-   If the layer has no media, the apps dialogue will open. Otherwise, click on Add New.
-   Go to “General Apps”.
-   Choose “Windy.com”.
-   Paste the copied HTML Embed Code in the designated field.
-   Check the “Enable” checkbox to allow the web app to auto-reload every X minutes. Specify the desired refresh interval in minutes.
-   Check the “Enable” checkbox to allow zooming in the web app. Use the scroll feature right and left to zoom in and out (%).
-   Click “Save” and then “Publish”.

Now, any updates to the weather will automatically reflect on your digital signage screen within the refresh rate minutes.
