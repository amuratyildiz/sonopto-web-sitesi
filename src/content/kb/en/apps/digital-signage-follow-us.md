-   Go to the Management Console.
-   Click on the playlist then the layer where you will add Follow us.
-   If the layer has no media, the apps dialog will open. Otherwise, click on **Add New**
-   Click on **Follow Us**
-   Click on the social media you want to create a banner for
-   Enter the account (that will be displayed on the banner)
-   Enter the URL (used to generate the QR code)
-   Check QR code if you need to show it
-   Click on **Save**
