-   Go to Youtube and choose the video or short you want to show.
-   Click on **Share**.
-   Click on **Copy** to copy the link.
-   Go to the Management Console.
-   Click on the layer where you will add Youtube.
-   If the layer has no media, the apps dialog will open. Otherwise, click on **Add New**
-   Click on **Google Apps**.
-   Then click on **Youtube**.
-   Paste the link you just copied.
-   Click on Save.

## YouTube Playlist:

To display **YouTube Playlists**, select the desired playlist, copy its URL, and paste it into the designated field to run it on the screen.

The playlist will begin playing automatically. Any new video added to that YouTube playlist will also play automatically in the next loop, keeping your content fresh without any additional effort on your part.
