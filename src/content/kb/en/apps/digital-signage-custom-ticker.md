Use the Custom ticker App to create a custom news feed with stories you write yourself.

This App can be used in 1920x120 layer only.

<video width="90%" src="/kb/apps/digital-signage-custom-ticker.mp4" controls="" preload="metadata" playsinline=""></video>

-   Go to the Management Console.
-   Click on the playlist then the 1920x120 layer where you want to add a Custom ticker
-   If the layer has no media, the apps dialog will open. Otherwise, click on **Add New**
-   Click on **Text Tickers**
-   Click on **Custom ticker**
-   Click on Edit
-   Type in the stories
-   Click on **Done**
-   Choose the number of stories
-   Choose the text color for your ticker
-   Choose the background color for your ticker
-   Add a digital clock
-   Adjust the ticker speed: 1 is the slowest, 10 is the fastest
-   Choose your scroll direction, default is left to right
-   Click on **Save**
