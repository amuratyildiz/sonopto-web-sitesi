A group allows you to schedule playlists for multiple screens at the same time.

<video width="90%" src="/kb/screens/digital-signage-group.mp4" controls="" preload="metadata" playsinline=""></video>

-   Open the management console, and navigate to screens page
-   Click on groups tab
-   Click on **Add Group**
-   Type in the group name and choose horizontal or vertical
-   Click on **Save**
-   Click on **Set Media Source**
-   Choose a playlist or a schedule for this group
-   Go to screens page
-   Click on the media source of your screen
-   Instead of assigning a playlist, Click on groups in the dialog and assign your screen to the group we just created
-   Click on Groups Tab again, and click on screens near the group name: you can find the screen we just added to this group
-   You can add more screens to the group
