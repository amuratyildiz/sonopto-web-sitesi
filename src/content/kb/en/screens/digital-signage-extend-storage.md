An external SD Card can be used to extend the internal storage of a screen to store apps, files and media for large digital signage playlists.

This feature can be used on screens with Android 8.0 and above that have an SD Card slot.

On your screen, open Settings:

To open Settings:

-   Remote control: Press on Menu or Settings or long press for 3 seconds on arrow down
-   Keyboard: Alt+M or F5
-   Touch Screen: Long Press on the top left corner

<img src="/destek/screens/password.webp" alt="Password" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

-   Enter the screen’s password and hit Enter

<img src="/destek/screens/open_settings.webp" alt="open Settings" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

-   Press on **Open Settings**

<img src="/destek/screens/settings.webp" alt="Settings" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

-   Press on Storage

<img src="/destek/screens/sdcard.webp" alt="SD CARD" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

-   Make sur eyour SD Card is inserted, it might show as incomaptible, if so, press on it to format it
    
-   Otherwise, by pressing on it it will open and you can view the files
    

<img src="/destek/screens/storage_settings.webp" alt="Storage Settings" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

-   Go to the three dots menu on the top right and press on it
-   Press on **Storage Settings**

<img src="/destek/screens/format_as_internal.webp" alt="Format as Internal" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

-   Press on **Format as Internal**

<img src="/destek/screens/format.webp" alt="Format" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

-   Note that the SD CARD content will be totally erased
-   Press on **Format SD CARD**

<img src="/destek/screens/formatting.webp" alt="Formatting" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

-   Wait for the format to finish, do not eject it or turn of your screen while formatting

<img src="/destek/screens/continue.webp" alt="Continue" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

-   Press on Continue

<img src="/destek/screens/move_content.webp" alt="Move" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

-   Press on Move Content

<img src="/destek/screens/done.webp" alt="Done" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

-   Press on Done

<img src="/destek/screens/internal_sd_card.webp" alt="Internal SD CARD" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

-   Your SD Card will show as an internal SD Card that can be used to extend your digital signage media storage

### View your storage

-   Go to the management console
-   Click on Screens
-   Click on the settings of your screen
-   Click on your screen details

<img src="/destek/screens/screen_details.webp" alt="Screen Details" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

-   Check if the total storage shows your newly extended storage
