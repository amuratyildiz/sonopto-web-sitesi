To change the management console language:

-   Click on the language icon in the top navigation menu
-   Click on one of the languages
-   The system will reload with the new language applied

_The admin console supports 16 languages:_

_English - 中文 - Español - عربى - Português - русский - Deutsch - Türkçe - Français - Italiano - Polski - Nederlands - Magyar - ελληνικά - Svenska - Hrvatski_
